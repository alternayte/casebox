import { Card, CardGrid } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

This site documents Casebox 0.1.0: how to install it, use it on your team's repositories, and operate it.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="The steering report" icon="magnifier">
    How often people correct their agents, where, and why, from the session logs already on your machines.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Patterns" icon="document">
    Corrections that share a cause become a pattern, with the quotes behind it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Proposals you approve" icon="approve-check">
    For each pattern, one small change: an instruction, a skill, an MCP server, or a code note for your agent. At most three wait at once.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Private until you share" icon="pencil">
    `casebox apply` writes an approved change privately, so you gather evidence before you ask your team. 30 days later, Casebox shows whether the corrections fell.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

This site documents Casebox 0.1.0: how to install it, use it on your team's repositories, and operate it.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="The steering report" icon="magnifier">
    How often people correct their agents, where, and why, from the session logs already on your machines.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cases from your history" icon="document">
    Merged pull requests, reverts and corrected sessions become replayable tasks with tests that decide them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Measured comparisons" icon="approve-check">
    A model, agent or harness change runs on your cases and returns better, worse, equivalent or inconclusive, with an interval.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Proposals as pull requests" icon="pencil">
    Recurring corrections become small harness edits, tested on held-out cases before a person merges them.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

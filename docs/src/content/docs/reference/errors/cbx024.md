This page says what CBX024 means and how you fix it.

## What happened

Casebox shows the cost of an evaluation before it runs, from the `prices` table of casebox.yml. There is no live price lookup, and a model without a price is refused.

## What to do

Add the model under `prices` in USD per million tokens: `input`, `output`, and optionally `cache_read` and `cache_write`, from your contract with the provider.

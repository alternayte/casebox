This page says what CBX031 means and how you fix it.

## What happened

The estimate is over the cap of one evaluation: the organisation's cap per evaluation, or the lower `--cap` you gave.

## What to do

Use fewer cases or repeats, give a higher `--cap` up to the organisation's cap, or ask an Admin to raise the cap.

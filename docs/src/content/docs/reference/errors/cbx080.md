This page says what CBX080 means and how you fix it.

## What happened

Casebox shows a theme, pattern, quote or count only when at least k mapped people are behind it (default 3), so no one's work stands out.

## What to do

Nothing to fix: the group appears once more people's data reaches it. Admins set k (at least 2) in the privacy settings; unmapped people never count.
